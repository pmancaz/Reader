Reading on the original website

A saved reading copy can focus on the article. The original website keeps its own navigation, colours and layout. Both views can be useful when returning to a paper, looking at a figure in context, or following a link from the publisher.

This is original local example content for checking the Reader and Original toggle. It has no account, external service or personal document. Keeping the saved reading copy separate means that browsing a website cannot replace the article, highlights or notes already kept in the library.

A place for the source

A useful reader can make room for both the source and a quieter reading view. The important details should survive switching between them, including the reader's place in the saved text and the notes that prompted a return to it.
